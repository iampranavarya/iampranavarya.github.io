Questions

Good to know.

What does the process look like?
A quick call, then a concept, moodboard and shot list for your approval. Nothing is produced until you approve it. Then production, edit and delivery in every format you need.
Can you combine real footage and AI?
Yes. Many brand films use a real shoot for people and product, and AI for scenes that would be too expensive or impossible to film.
Do you work outside Germany?
Yes. We have filmed in 11 countries, from Singapore to St. Moritz.
How is a brand film priced?
Every brand campaign is quoted on scope, shoot days, deliverables and usage rights. Share your budget range and we agree on a number within it.
