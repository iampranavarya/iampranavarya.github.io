AI video · Jewellery

AI films for jewellery.

Jewellery sells in the detail: the sparkle, the hand, the moment the box opens. AI films let jewellers show pieces in macro and in mood without a specialist macro set-up or a model day.

Get a film like this WhatsApp
Where it works

Built for how jewellery brands sell.

  • Product videos for your online shop
  • Engagement and gifting season campaigns
  • Instagram Reels for new pieces
  • In-store and window screens
What we need from you

Three things to get started.

  • Sharp photos of each piece
  • Materials and stones to highlight
  • The occasion or season you are selling for

You approve the concept and script before anything is produced. Every film is delivered in vertical 9:16, square and widescreen.

How it works

From idea to premiere in four steps.

  1. 01

    Quick call

    Tell us about your project, your audience and where the film will run.

  2. 02

    Concept & plan

    You get a concept and script or shot list. Nothing is produced until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Production

    AI films are directed shot by shot with the latest generative video tools. Real shoots: my team comes on location.

  4. 04

    Edit & delivery

    Editing, colour and sound design, delivered in every format you need, with feedback rounds included.

Questions
