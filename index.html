Questions

Good to know.

Real shoot or AI film: which do I need?
If it's about real people and real moments (an event, a wedding, your team, your venue), go for a real shoot. If you want a big-budget look for a product, brand or place without the logistics, AI is perfect. Not sure? Tell me what it's for and I'll recommend, and many projects combine both.
What do you need from me?
For a shoot: the date, location and what matters most to you. For an AI film: your logo, a few photos of your product or venue, and a short description of your brand and audience. I take it from there.
Will an AI film really look like my brand?
Yes. Your name, logo, colours and products are built into the concept before production starts, and you approve the script first.
How long does it take?
Event edits are usually delivered within 72 hours, with an optional same-day teaser. AI films are much faster than a traditional production. You get a clear delivery date after our first call.
Which formats do I get?
Whatever you need: vertical 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 4:5 and 1:1 for feeds and ads, 16:9 for YouTube, websites and screens.
Do you work outside Berlin?
Absolutely. We've filmed in 11 countries, from Singapore to St. Moritz, and AI films are produced remotely for clients anywhere in the world.
