The Portable Power Station That Changed My Remote Shoots

By Pranav Arya · PAFP · #gear · #gadgets · #power · #remote-shoots

A decent chunk of my work happens somewhere without reliable power, a remote location shoot, a festival site with limited outlet access, a multi-day event where the crew room outlet is shared by twenty other people's chargers. A compact power station solved a problem I'd been managing badly with a pile of loose power banks for years.

Why loose power banks weren't actually working

Individual power banks for cameras, lights, a laptop, and a phone meant carrying five different devices with five different charge states to track, and inevitably the one thing I actually needed was the one that had been left half-charged. Consolidating into a single power station with multiple outputs, including a real AC outlet for anything that doesn't take USB-C, removed that specific failure mode entirely.

What actually matters in the spec sheet

Capacity gets the marketing focus, and it matters less than output flexibility for how I actually use one. A unit with a real AC pass-through outlet handles a laptop charger or a small LED panel without an adapter, which matters more day to day than an extra few hundred watt-hours of capacity I rarely fully drain anyway. Weight is the other real trade-off nobody advertises honestly, more capacity is heavier, and I'd rather carry a smaller unit and recharge more often than one so heavy it stays in the car.

  • Real AC output matters more than raw capacity for mixed gear
  • Weight is the honest trade-off against capacity, plan for it
  • Solar charging add-ons are nice in theory, rarely fast enough to matter on a working day

Where it earns its keep most

The single most valuable use case has turned out to be keeping camera batteries topped up during a long multi-day shoot without hunting for an outlet every night, which sounds minor and has genuinely removed a recurring source of low-grade stress from remote jobs. Not an exciting piece of gear to write about. It's the kind of unglamorous investment that quietly prevents an actual production problem.
