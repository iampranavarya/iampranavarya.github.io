Sigma fp2: The Tiny Full-Frame Camera Filmmakers Have Been Waiting For

Sigma's fp2 adds a partially stacked sensor, phase detection AF and 6K open gate ProRes RAW to a 376g body. Where it fits in a working filmmaker's kit.

Pranav AryaOctober 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Sigma announced the fp2 on October 23, the long-awaited successor to the original fp from 2019. Sigma calls it the world's smallest full-frame camera, and on paper it fixes almost everything that made the first fp frustrating on real shoots.

What changed

  • Sensor: a 24.5MP full-frame, partially stacked CMOS, which should mean faster readout and less rolling shutter than the original.
  • Video: 6K open gate recording with Apple ProRes RAW, plus 32-bit float audio.
  • Autofocus: phase detection AF, the single biggest upgrade for anyone who shot run-and-gun with the old contrast-detect fp.
  • Storage: 70GB built in, so you can keep shooting without a card in a pinch.
  • Size: 376g body only, 460g with battery and card.
  • Stills: up to 20fps continuous shooting and a tilting screen.

Pricing is $2,199 body only, and $2,749 with either the EVF-21 viewfinder or the 50mm F2 DG Contemporary lens. It goes on sale on November 11. There were rumours of a built-in viewfinder; it did not happen, so the EVF stays an add-on.

Where it fits

I would not see this as an A-camera for long event days. It is a modular camera that needs accessories to be comfortable: a cage, a grip, a monitor, external power for long takes. That is the point of the design, but it means the real-world price is higher than the body.

Where it gets interesting is as a second or third camera:

  • Crash cam and tight spaces. Inside a car, on a bar counter, rigged to a door frame. Full-frame image quality in a body that fits where a cinema camera never will.
  • Gimbal work. Light enough for small gimbals without counterweights.
  • Matching an A-cam in the grade. ProRes RAW gives you room to match it to a bigger camera in post, which matters when the B-cam footage ends up next to the hero shots.
  • Stills on the same shoot. With the new AF and 20fps, it is a credible hybrid for content days where you need both photos and video.

The competition

At $2,199 it lands right next to compact cinema bodies like the Nikon ZR, which costs the same body only and brings RED colour and a full set of video tools. The ZR is the more complete video camera out of the box. The fp2 is the smaller, more modular one, with the Sigma colour that many people bought the original for. Choose based on how you rig, not the spec sheet.

Should you wait for reviews?

Yes. Heat management, battery life with 6K RAW, and how the new AF actually tracks will decide whether this is a workhorse or a niche tool. Those are things only real-world testing will answer. But for small crews who keep asking for full-frame quality in the smallest possible package, Sigma has finally built the camera the original fp promised.
