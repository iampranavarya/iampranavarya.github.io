What I'd Actually Ask For This Year, as a Working Filmmaker

A no-nonsense holiday gift guide for filmmakers and photographers, built from gear I actually use rather than a generic sponsored list.

Pranav AryaDecember 1, 2025 · 2 min read

Gift guides for creators tend to be either thinly disguised affiliate lists or so expensive they're useless as actual gift suggestions for anyone who isn't buying themselves a new camera body. This is neither. It's the small, genuinely useful stuff that's actually sat in my bag this year, at prices a friend or family member could realistically manage.

Under fifty euros, genuinely useful

A good lens cloth sounds like a joke gift until you've watched someone smear a five-thousand-euro lens with a shirt sleeve mid-shoot. A proper cleaning kit with a blower, a microfiber cloth, and a cleaning pen covers ninety percent of the maintenance most gear ever actually needs, and almost nobody buys this for themselves until after something's already scratched.

A solid set of memory cards from a reputable brand, not the cheapest option in a multipack, is the single highest-value gift I can think of for anyone shooting seriously. Card failure is one of the few genuinely irreplaceable losses in this work, and most people underinvest in this exact spot to save a small amount of money.

Fifty to two hundred, the sweet spot

A small, quiet clamp-on light for quick interviews and product shots has become one of the most-used items in my bag this year, more than a full lighting kit some weeks, purely because it's small enough to actually bring along on jobs where I'd otherwise skip lighting entirely. A dedicated audio recorder or a decent lavalier mic kit falls in this range too, and audio quality is where amateur work gets separated from professional work faster than any visual element.

  • Under 50: proper cleaning kit, quality memory cards
  • 50 to 200: clamp-on LED light, lavalier mic kit
  • 200 and up: a genuinely good camera strap or harness system, underrated every year

The gift nobody thinks to give

A cloud storage subscription, gifted for a full year, is the least glamorous item on this list and might be the most genuinely useful one for anyone whose backup habits are shakier than they should be. It's not exciting to unwrap. Ask anyone who's lost a shoot to a failed drive whether they'd have preferred the excitement or the backup, and you already know the answer.
