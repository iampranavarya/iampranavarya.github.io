I Switched Back to Primes for Event Coverage. Here's Why

By Pranav Arya · PAFP · #lenses · #cameras · #gear · #technique

A 24-70mm f/2.8 was my default event lens for years, the safe, flexible choice that covers most situations without a lens change. Over the past few months I've quietly gone back to shooting a lot of event work on a small set of primes instead, and it's changed both how the work looks and, more surprisingly, how I actually move through a room.

Constraint forces better positioning

A zoom lets you stay in one spot and reframe with your wrist, which is convenient and, I've realized, was making me lazier about actually moving through a space to find a better angle. A 35mm or 50mm prime with no zoom ring forces me to walk toward or away from a subject to change the frame, and that physical movement consistently finds better angles than standing still and twisting a zoom ring would have. It's a small behavioral shift with a real effect on the actual images.

The light gathering difference matters more than I expected

Most event spaces are dimmer than they look to the naked eye, and the extra stop or more of light a fast prime gathers over even a good zoom means real, usable shutter speeds in rooms where a zoom would push ISO into mushy territory. That difference shows up constantly in delivered work, especially anything shot at dusk or in a poorly lit venue, which describes a genuinely large share of event spaces.

  • Primes force better physical positioning, not just a sharper image
  • The extra light gathering matters most in genuinely dim, real venues
  • A zoom still wins for fast-changing, unpredictable single-camera coverage

Where I still reach for a zoom

Solo coverage of something fast and unpredictable, a ceremony, a live performance where I can't reposition freely, still goes to a zoom because missing the moment while changing lenses or repositioning is worse than a slightly softer or dimmer frame. For the controlled chaos of a brand event or a fashion show where I can move freely, primes have earned back their spot in the main bag, and I don't think I'm going back to defaulting to a zoom anytime soon.
