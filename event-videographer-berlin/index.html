Questions

Good to know.

How fast do we get the event video?
Event edits are usually delivered within 72 hours, and a same-day teaser is available so you can post while the event is still trending.
Do you cover events outside Berlin?
Yes. We are based in Berlin and have filmed in 11 countries, from Singapore to St. Moritz.
Can you also take photos at the event?
Yes. We offer event photography alongside video, delivered edited and ready to post.
How is pricing calculated?
Event coverage is quoted at a day or half-day rate plus deliverables. Share your budget range and we agree on a number that works for both sides.
